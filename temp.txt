&nbsp;Instant Dawoodi Bohra Style Gujarati Khaman
















Ingredients:





Gram Flour(Besan): 150gms

Sugar: 20gms

Tumeric(Haldi) powder: 1 tea spoon

Lemon (Limbu): 1 Piece

Salt

Green Chilli (Leela Marcha): 3 Pieces

Curry Leaves (Curry Patta)

Mustard Seeds

Oil

Eno Powder: Small Packet

Tamaru Dawoodi Bohra style Khaman taiyar chhe!







Steps in English:





Take a bowl and add gram flour (besan) to it.






Add salt according to taste.






Then add sugar to the mixture.






Add turmeric (haldi) powder.






Mix everything well.






Add lemon juice and mix it well again.






Add some water and mix it to make a slightly thin batter. Stir continuously to ensure a smooth consistency.






Then add a small packet of Eno powder and again add a little water. Mix thoroughly.






Keep mixing well to activate the batter.






Take a plate and grease it lightly with oil. Pour the batter into this greased plate.






Take a large pot (tapeli) and heat water in it for steaming.






Place the plate with the batter inside the pot.






Cover the pot with a lid and let the Khaman steam for 20 minutes.








For the tempering (vaghar/tadka):







Take a pan and heat a little oil.






Add mustard seeds (raai) and curry leaves.






Add green chilies.






Add water.






Add a pinch of asafoetida (hing).






Let the steamed Khaman cool down.






Then pour the tempering over the Khaman.






Your 
Dawoodi Bohra Style Khaman
 is ready to serve!
